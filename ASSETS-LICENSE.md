## What this covers

- The After You name and logo, the app icon and the splash art: game/assets/icon.svg, game/assets/splash_icon.svg, game/assets/boot_splash.png and the logo, icon, banner and thumbnail files in docs/branding.
- Art and images: game/assets/chapter_thumbnails, game/assets/paid_levels, game/assets/ui/seed.svg, scripts/chapter_templates/placeholder-thumbnail.png, docs/screenshots (except docs/screenshots/photo-memory-android.png, whose shared photo has not been traced) and the rest of docs/branding, apart from the third-party marks named below.
- Icons drawn for After You: the SVG files directly in game/assets/ui and those in game/assets/ui/settings and game/assets/ui/whats_new, except the files named under Third-party files.
- Sound and music: every file in game/assets/audio.
- Level and chapter content and design: game/content/chapters, game/content/dressing, game/content/cosmetics, game/content/campaigns, game/content/compatibility, game/content/water_locks and the chapter definition files (.json) in backend/src/v2.
- Player-facing text: game/presentation/player_copy.gd, game/content/changelog.json, game/content/changelog_archive.json, game/content/anonymous_names.json and backend/src/public-policy.ts.
- 3D models and textures: the repository has no model or texture files today. The game builds its characters and scenery in code under game/presentation, and that code is under LICENSE. Model or texture files added later are covered here.
