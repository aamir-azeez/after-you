Terms: Google's Play Games Services branding guidelines, https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/branding,
and the Guidelines for Third Party Use of Google Brand Features they point to. In short, as read
on 2026-11-03:
- Games may use these branding elements without pre-approval when they follow the guidelines.
  Any other use of Google brands needs Google's written consent.
- The controller icon is the one Google asks for on buttons and other elements that open the
  default Play Games UI, such as a leaderboard.
- Games may change the icon's colour, texture and size to match their style, but must not
  substantially change its shape, cover it with other elements or put it on a busy background.
- Google's icons must not be used in the game's own app icon, and Google trademarks must not be
  used in the game's title. "Google Play Games" may be used nominatively in game text.
